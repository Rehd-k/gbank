Let’s learn, grow and share together.

As a community partner, we want all members of the community to succeed. We’re proud to offer you many options to educate yourself on the topics most important to you, as you work to meet your financial and career goals. We encourage you to share these resources with anyone you feel would benefit from attending a class or viewing our complimentary materials.

As part of Averon Trust Bank’s commitment to community education, we’ve partnered with CVENT to provide online scheduling and registration services. By clicking on a class, you will be redirected to the registration website hosted by CVENT. CVENT has agreed not to share the information you enter on your registration with anyone other than Averon Trust Bank. CVENT will only use the information it collects from your registration for scheduling you for class and sending information related to that class.

Senior Life

We want you to enjoy your golden years. To do so, it’s important to have your budget for the life you live today, plans in place if you (or your partner) pass away, an awareness of the many identity theft and financial scams used to prey on older adults, and of course, opportunities to connect with your peers, kids and grandkids. Consider registering to talk Budgeting, ID Theft for Older Adults, How to Use Your Cell Phone, Estate Planning, or joining one of our Book Clubs.
