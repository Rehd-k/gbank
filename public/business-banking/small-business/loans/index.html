SBA loans available through Averon Trust Bank’s Commercial team

We proudly offer a wide range of SBA financing programs, such as 7(a) and 504, as well as programs for exporters, contractors, and other trades, to help small businesses start and grow their operations.

Averon Trust Bank’s Preferred Lenders Program (PLP) status streamlines the process for providing business financing to the small business community. PLP lenders have demonstrated a proficiency in processing and servicing SBA-guaranteed loans. To learn more, contact a member of our Commercial Lending team below. 

Small Business Loans 

We’ve also developed three streamlined small business loan products, available directly through our branches or cash management team, for those in need of fast access to capital. 

Small Business Professional Credit Line — for those looking to borrow $25,000 – $100,000 — is appropriate for commercial borrowers, including corporations, LLCs and PCs.

Small Business Overdraft Credit Line — for those looking to borrow $2,500 – $25,000 — is a good choice for sole proprietorships. 

Small Business Fixed Asset Acquisition Term Loan — for those looking to borrow up to $50,000. It can be combined with either of the small business credit lines. 

To learn more, contact your local branch or call 678-465-8306.
